### Hi, I'm Joshua 👋

I'm a recent Computer Science graduate from Purdue University, currently a visiting researcher in the [Sabuncu Lab](https://sabuncu.engineering.cornell.edu/) at Cornell under Dr. Heejong Kim. I work on deep learning for medical imaging, with a focus on building tools that work reliably across the patients who actually need them.

#### What I work on

My research sits at the intersection of computer vision, interpretability, and clinical deployment. I care less about pushing a number on a benchmark and more about whether a model holds up across subpopulations, imaging conditions, and the messy realities of clinical data.

A few threads I'm pulling on:

- **Longitudinal medical imaging.** At Cornell, I'm exploring interpretability and representation learning for models that reason over scans across time, using the LILAC framework alongside transformer-based attention (ViT, Swin) and saliency methods like Grad-CAM and CDAM.
- **Echocardiographic aortic root assessment.** At Purdue, I built a U-Net pipeline for aortic root segmentation in pediatric Marfan syndrome patients, in collaboration with Riley Hospital for Children. The project taught me where single-modality imaging hits its ceiling, and now shapes my interest in multimodal approaches.
- **Equitable digital health.** Through Healobi, I'm developing a platform that explores multimodal biomarkers (voice, facial signals, text) to extend mental health support to underserved communities.

#### A bit more

I previously served as an Information System Platoon Leader in the Republic of Korea Marine Corps, which is where I learned most of what I know about leading teams under constraint. Outside of research, I lead a small group at Littlespark Korean Christian Fellowship.
